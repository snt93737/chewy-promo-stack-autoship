## How the Autoship discount really works (the 35% is a one-off)

Autoship is Chewy's subscription delivery program, and it's where the headline numbers come from. Chewy's own terms spell it out: new customers establishing their first Autoship subscription get a 35% promotional discount on the initial order, capped at $20. Every delivery after that drops to 5%. Some sites quote Autoship as a standing 35% discount — that's the welcome rate, and it applies exactly once.

You choose the delivery frequency at checkout (the button says "Yes, make my life easy"), and you can pause, skip, reschedule or cancel anytime from your account with no fee. That flexibility makes the one-time 35% usable even if you have zero interest in recurring deliveries — though cancelling immediately after the discounted order lands is exactly the behavior retailer terms are written against, so read yours before you do it.

Two Autoship variants pay out even better:

- **Pharmacy Autoship** gives new users 50% off the first order. You add the prescription at checkout, fill in your pet and vet info, and Chewy's Rx team verifies the prescription with your vet before shipping within 3–5 business days. The pharmacy covers flea and tick preventives, joint and allergy meds, dental care and more across dogs, cats, birds, reptiles, small pets and horses.
- **Chewy's Get Real fresh dog food line** gives new subscribers 50% off their first full order after a short questionnaire. Flavors include Chicken and Brussels Sprouts, Beef and Sweet Potato, and Turkey and Cranberry, and you can mix them in one order.

A quick illustrative math check for a first order: a $50 Autoship basket drops to $32.50 with the standard 35% welcome rate (the $20 cap doesn't bite until your would-be savings exceed it). If a code like DEAL10 also applies, that's another $10 off, and the whole order can still earn cash back.
